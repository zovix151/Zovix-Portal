Explore the AI tools

ZOVIX brings specialized creation workflows into one online studio. Choose a tool for the task, provide a prompt or media where requested, then review and refine the result.

Cinematic Engine

Build cinematic video projects from a concept or script, with scene-by-scene visuals, narration, and optional music workflows.

Face Studio

Use portrait media and a script to create talking-character and face video content. Review consent and usage rights for any person depicted.

Creative Workshop

Generate creative visual concepts and images from written prompts for campaigns, stories, and moodboards.

Blueprints

Explore architectural and product concepts with blueprint-style visuals and design planning. Generated plans are conceptual and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before construction.

Upscaler

Enhance image detail and create an upscaled version of an uploaded image.

Draw

Turn written ideas into art, sketches, and illustration concepts.

Video Editor

Work with uploaded media to edit and assemble video projects, then review the finished output.

AI Agent

Get AI assistance for drafting creative and social content tasks from a brief or topic.

AI Sales

Develop product-focused sales video concepts and marketing content for a chosen audience or funnel.

Live Emotion

Generate expressive voice content by combining text with available voice and emotion settings.

AI outputs can contain mistakes or require editing. Review all generated content, verify rights and factual claims, and use a qualified professional for technical plans or safety-critical decisions.
