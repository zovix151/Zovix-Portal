Face Generation & Likeness Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy applies when you upload, capture, generate, edit, or share a face, portrait, voice, or other identifying likeness using ZOVIX face and talking-character tools. It supplements the ZOVIX terms and other applicable policies.

You are responsible for your use of the tools and outputs. Do not upload or generate a person's likeness unless you have the rights and permissions required by law. A person's fame, public profile, or availability of an image online does not itself give you permission.

1. Permission and lawful rights

You must own or have all necessary rights, permissions, and consents for every image, video, voice recording, script, and other material you submit. Obtain the person's informed permission where required, including permission to create and use a synthetic or altered likeness. You are responsible for checking privacy, publicity/personality, copyright, data-protection, and other applicable laws.

These requirements apply to celebrities, public figures, private individuals, and people you know. Do not assume that a public image, public appearance, or an existing online video grants permission to make a talking or altered version.

2. Uses that are not allowed

You may not use ZOVIX to create, request, or distribute face or voice content that:

  • Uses a real person's likeness without permission where permission is required, or violates that person's legal rights.
  • Falsely depicts a person saying or doing something, endorsing a product, or participating in an event in a way likely to mislead others, including impersonation, fraud, or election interference.
  • Is intimate, sexual, or exploitative content involving a real person without their explicit consent, or any sexual content involving a minor. Do not upload or depict minors in sexualized or exploitative contexts.
  • Harasses, threatens, humiliates, defames, discriminates against, or otherwise targets a person for harm.
  • Violates any applicable law, court order, or another person's rights.

3. Your responsibility

You—not ZOVIX—are responsible for the materials you submit, the prompts and instructions you provide, the content generated from them, and how you edit, publish, or distribute that content. To the extent permitted by applicable law, you are responsible for claims, losses, or harm arising from your unauthorized or unlawful use of another person's likeness.

Where content could reasonably be mistaken for authentic footage, speech, or endorsement, clearly disclose that it was generated or altered using AI. Review outputs before sharing; AI results can be inaccurate or misleading.

4. ZOVIX response to violations

ZOVIX may restrict or disable access, remove or block content where available, and investigate reports of suspected violations. We may take further action and cooperate with lawful requests as required by law. To report suspected misuse, contact zovixenterprises@gmail.com and include the relevant content or account details that you can lawfully share.

5. No blanket waiver of legal duties

ZOVIX provides tools for user-directed creation and does not certify that an upload, prompt, or output is authorized or lawful. Nothing in this policy removes rights or excludes or limits obligations or liability that cannot legally be excluded or limited. This policy is not a substitute for a complete Terms of Service or for legal advice about a particular use.

6. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Questions or reports can be sent to zovixenterprises@gmail.com.
